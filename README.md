## 🧠 The Idea

J.G-TEEN turns emotional intelligence into an interactive experience. The **Mind Library** uses stories, choices, reflection and consequences to encourage teenagers to think about resilience, empathy, courage and decision-making.

## ⚡ Experience Map

```text
Story Library
      ↓
Choose a scenario
      ↓
Make a decision
      ↓
See consequences
      ↓
Reflect on the outcome
      ↓
Build emotional awareness
```

- 📚 30+ interactive story experiences
